Frequently asked questions.

Need more information? Send us your question and we'll help clarify the details.

Do you accept bookings across Australia?

We welcome enquiries from all Australian states and territories. Please provide your suburb and postcode so we can confirm whether cleaning services are available in your area.

How much does bond cleaning cost?

The advertised options are $99 per hour and a $1,111 fixed quote. The final arrangement depends on the property, cleaning scope and confirmed availability.

How do I request a quote?

Complete the quote form on this page. You'll be directed to our existing booking form to submit your details.

Can I request a deep clean?

Yes, you can request deep cleaning through the quote form. Include your property details and preferred date so your request can be assessed.

Is my booking confirmed when I request a quote?

No. A quote request is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking. Your location, pricing, service scope and availability need to be confirmed before the booking is finalised.
